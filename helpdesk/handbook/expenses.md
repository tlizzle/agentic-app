# Expenses

Rules for spending company money and getting reimbursed at Acme Corp.

## Spending limits

Meals while travelling are capped at 40 USD per person per day.
Hotels are capped at 180 USD per night; book through the travel portal where possible.
Any single expense above 500 USD needs written approval from your manager before you spend it.

## What is covered

Travel to client sites, conference tickets, books and online courses related to your role, and team meals approved by your manager.
Alcohol, personal entertainment and fines (such as parking tickets) are never reimbursed.

## How to claim

Submit claims in the Expenses app within 30 days of the purchase date.
Attach a photo of the itemised receipt for every item over 10 USD.
Choose a category and add a one-line business reason.

## Reimbursement timeline

Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll run.
Claims submitted after the 20th of the month are paid the following month.

## Corporate cards

Managers and frequent travellers can request a corporate credit card from Finance.
Card transactions must still be submitted in the Expenses app with receipts.
