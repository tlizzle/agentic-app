# Leave Policy

This document covers paid time off for all full-time employees.

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee receives 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues monthly from your start date and is pro-rated in your first year.
Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year; they expire on 31 March.

## How to request leave

Submit requests in the HR portal (People > Time Off) at least 5 working days in advance.
Your direct manager approves or declines within 2 working days.
Requests of 10 or more consecutive days need approval from your manager and department head.

## Sick leave

You get 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
Tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day you are out.
A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Public holidays

The company observes the public holidays of the country where your contracted office is located.
If a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following Monday is a day off.
