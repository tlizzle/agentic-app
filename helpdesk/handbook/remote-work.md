# Remote Work

Acme Corp's hybrid working arrangement.

## Hybrid schedule

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
Everyone is expected in their home office on Tuesdays and Thursdays unless their manager agrees otherwise.
Fully remote arrangements need written approval from your department head and HR.

## Office locations

Acme has three offices: Taipei (headquarters, Xinyi District), London (Shoreditch) and Austin (downtown).
Your home office is the one named in your employment contract.
You may work from any Acme office when travelling; book a desk in the Workplace app.

## Working from another country

Working from a country other than your contracted one is allowed for up to 20 days per year.
Tell HR at least 2 weeks before you travel so tax and insurance can be checked.

## Home office setup

Acme pays a one-time home office allowance of 300 USD for a desk and chair.
Claim it through the Expenses app under the category "Home office".

## Core hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in your home office's local time; be reachable on Slack during that window.
