# IT Equipment

How Acme Corp issues, replaces and supports company hardware.

## Standard laptop

New employees receive a company laptop on their first day: a 14-inch MacBook Pro or a Dell Latitude.
Laptops come with 16 GB of RAM by default; 32 GB is available for engineering roles on request.

## Laptop refresh cycle

Laptops are replaced every 3 years, counted from the date the device was issued.
IT contacts you one month before your refresh date to choose a replacement model.
You may keep your old laptop for up to 2 weeks to migrate data, then return it to IT.

## Reporting hardware problems

Report any hardware fault by opening an IT support ticket in the helpdesk.
Include your device's asset tag (the sticker on the underside) and a short description of the problem.
IT responds within 1 business day. Loaner laptops are available from the IT desk on floor 3 while repairs take place.

## Accessories and peripherals

Each employee may claim one monitor, one keyboard and one mouse for home use.
Order accessories through the IT portal; delivery takes 3 to 5 working days.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen device to IT immediately, within 24 hours, so the device can be remotely wiped.
